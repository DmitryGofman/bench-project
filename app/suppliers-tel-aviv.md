# Folding picnic bench (2 m): where to buy in Tel Aviv / Gush Dan

Research date: 2026-10-13. I searched in English and Hebrew.

## How to read the evidence column

- **[S] (seen in a search snippet):** The detail comes from the shop's own page as the search index showed it. I could not open the page live, because direct page fetches were blocked in my research environment. Prices and stock can be stale, so call or check the link before you go.
- **[I] (inference):** My own suggestion or reasoning. No page confirmed it.
- Prices are in ₪ and include VAT unless marked otherwise.

## Main findings

1. **The best single fastener source is Galboreg (גלבורג) in Rishon LeZion.** Its online shop lists A4/316 M12×80 hex bolts, M12 and M8 nyloc nuts, flat washers, R-clips and spring pins. It delivers in the centre of Israel.
2. **Israeli "2×4" is 45×95 mm or 42×92 mm, not 38×89.** Local timber yards sell planed treated pine as 45/95 (Shults, Ra'anana) or 42×92 (Gueta Avigdor, Rishon LeZion). I found no listing for 38×89 mm. Either re-dimension the drawings to 45×95, or ask the yard to plane the stock down to size.
3. **A 12 mm stainless ball-lock pin exists locally.** Camping Life sells a Ø12 stainless ball-lock pin, 90 mm to the balls, for about ₪65. The seller is in Or Akiva, about 50 km north of Tel Aviv, and ships nationwide.
4. **Not found anywhere:** an Israeli listing for oil-impregnated bronze bushings 18×22, or for 3 mm UHMW sheet. Call the bearing houses and plastics suppliers named in sections 4 and 5.
5. **Not found:** an Israeli fastener branch of Würth, Fischer or SPAX. The only "Würth Israel" in the results is Würth Elektronik in Caesarea, which sells electronic components. SPAX screws are listed by the Etz HaMahlef timber yard.

**Design note [I]:** OD18 × 2.0 gives a 14 mm bore. On an M12 bolt that leaves 2 mm of diametral clearance, so there is more play than with the 13 mm bore you specified. Either accept it, or wrap the bolt with a thin 12×14 sleeve. Another option is a 316 rod turned and bored by a local machine shop (מסגרייה / מחרטה).

**Note [I]:** No Israeli page I found lists a single **22 mm** or **36 mm** Forstner bit.
- **36 mm counterbore:** 35 mm is the common size and usually works for an M12 washer (Ø24) plus socket clearance. Check that your socket's OD is below 35 mm.
- **22 mm bushing bore:** For a press fit on a 22 mm OD bushing, a 22 mm bit is right. If none is available, use a 22 mm bi-metal hole saw with a pilot bit, which plumbing and HVAC shops carry. The hole is slightly rougher.
