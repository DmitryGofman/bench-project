# Simple garden bench — fabrication register

All dimensions mm. Generated from the current design.

## All timber
All ends square; no hidden laps, rebates or compound mitres. Dimensions are finished stock sizes. Break exposed edges 2–3 mm.

## Frame joints
Rails butt against posts and bearer ends butt against long rails. Brackets are required against racking; timber solids do not model screw holes or bracket plates.

## Seat board drilling
Two holes per support, across each board width at 20 mm from its edges. Support stations measured from inner seat left end: 22.5 (end support), 535, 1048 mm (end support). Move end holes onto actual side rail centre lines; do not drill at the board end.

## Back board drilling
Upright centre lines at 22.5 and 1137.5 mm from board left end. Two screws per upright, 20 mm from each long edge.

## Design scope
Reference-inspired new frame geometry. Connection capacity, species/grade and user loading require workshop verification; no tested load rating is claimed.
